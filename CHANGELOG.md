## 0.8.0

- Let users choose separate running and error colors for light and dark themes.
- Adjust the classic and botanical intensity preset widths while preserving saved custom widths.
- Smooth the terminal trunk-to-branch junction and refine the botanical crown and soil.
- Replace README previews with a high-resolution real-conversation GIF and comparisons of all three styles, themes and states.

- Draw a straight trunk in classic and botanical; the old wavy default (4.83 px) migrates to 0, custom values are kept.
- Top-level tool branches leave the trunk between rows instead of from the previous dot, so siblings no longer read as children.
- One dot rule everywhere: solid for thinking, ring for tools, same size at every depth, drawn in the line's own colour.
- Even row rhythm: top-level steps and nested calls share one 16 px gap; branch transitions bend within it instead of adding padding.
- Classic lines use the host's text colour at 38% strength, clearly lighter than labels; the taper is stronger along the trunk, stems and forks.
- Classic drops the ink texture and lightens forks (root fusion 0.7 → 0.3); the untouched old classic preset migrates, custom values and botanical wood are kept.
- Classic leaves are sage, the tree's one accent colour.
- Botanical crown is a small emblem (about 70 px instead of about 215 px) on a short neck, and never extends past the content's left edge; saved full-size crowns migrate to the emblem.
- Botanical roots get the crown's lit-wood shading; the stray root-hair arcs beside the trunk are removed.
- Settings lead with three choices: style cards with thumbnails, intensity (subtle / standard / vivid: line tone, width and dots together) and one animation switch. Everything else moves into a collapsed Advanced section; edits there show intensity as custom.
- Fix settings that had stopped working: the trunk-to-branch transition again sets the branch curve's height (0–48 px). Controls a style ignores are hidden there: transitions and bends in quiet, texture type and leaves in botanical, and dot scaling unless lines are wider than 3 px.
- Classic leaves now mean something: a leaf sprouts when a tool step finishes, and running or failed steps stay bare. The random leaf-density slider is gone; a saved density of 0 migrates to hidden leaves. Default leaf size is 9 px.
- Quiet lines are presented as the understated mode: listed last in the settings and READMEs.
